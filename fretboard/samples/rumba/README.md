# Rumba flamenca

The cajón and handclaps come from FreePats World Percussion. Xavimart, Gonzalo and Roberto recorded the flamenco cajón with two Zoom H1 recorders. Roberto recorded the handclaps with an AKG condenser microphone. Both sets of recordings have a CC0 dedication. [FreePats recording notes](https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Percussion/world-and-rare-percussion.html)

The numbers in the filenames are the publisher’s percussion keys. Key 50 supplies the low cajón stroke, 48 the tap, 49 the sharp slap and 59 the handclaps. Each stroke cycles through its own takes at their recorded pitch. The twelve Ogg files together use about 91 KiB. The original source URLs and hashes are in `sources.json`. Rebuild with `node tools/prepare-fretboard-instrument.mjs rumba` using Node, curl and FFmpeg.

Fretwise arranges individual nylon-string recordings into short muted strokes, upward brushes on the treble strings and accented downstrokes. The thumb plays the bass. In E Phrygian dominant the chords are Am → Dm → F → E. The rumba drummer places a low cajón hit on the “and” of three and leaves beat one open in the palmas part.
